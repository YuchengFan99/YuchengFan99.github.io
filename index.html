01About me

I am a PhD candidate in the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering at UNSW Sydney. Before that I was a research assistant at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou).

My research sits at the intersection of materials, mechanics and AI. I build micromechanics, molecular dynamics and finite element models that link nanofillers such as graphene and carbon nanotubes to the electrical, thermal and mechanical behaviour of concrete, and I use machine learning to accelerate prediction and design. The goal is infrastructure that monitors itself, melts snow, harvests energy and lasts longer with a lower carbon footprint.

I have published in Carbon, Cement and Concrete Composites, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, International Journal of Mechanical Sciences, Composite Structures and Soft Matter, and I review for Elsevier journals.
