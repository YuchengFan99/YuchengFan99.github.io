01About me

I am a PhD candidate in the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering at UNSW Sydney, supervised by Scientia Associate Professor Wengui Li and supported by the CSC–UNSW Joint Scholarship. I received my MSc from Nanjing Tech University under Prof. Chuang Feng, and worked as a research assistant at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). I am also the founder of Deep Composite Technology (Guangzhou) Co., Ltd.

My research sits at the intersection of materials, mechanics and AI. I build micromechanics, molecular dynamics and finite element models that link nanofillers such as graphene and carbon nanotubes to the electrical, thermal and mechanical behaviour of concrete, and I use machine learning to accelerate prediction and design. The goal is infrastructure that monitors itself, melts snow, harvests energy and lasts longer with a lower carbon footprint.

I have published 47 peer-reviewed journal papers, 36 of them in JCR Q1 journals, including first-author work in Carbon (one Clarivate Highly Cited Paper), Cement and Concrete Composites, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence and International Journal of Mechanical Sciences. I review for more than 30 international journals.
