PhD candidate

Yucheng Fan （樊宇澄）

AI for Science Multiscale modelling Smart cementitious composites

I’m a PhD candidate at the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering, UNSW Sydney, advised by Scientia Associate Professor Wengui Li and supported by the CSC–UNSW Joint Scholarship. I combine multiscale modelling, experiments and machine learning to design carbon nanomaterial reinforced concrete that can sense, heat, and harvest and store energy for smart, low-carbon infrastructure.

I received my master’s degree from Nanjing Tech University and my bachelor’s degree from Jiangsu University of Science and Technology, and was a research assistant at HKUST (Guangzhou). I’m also the founder of Deep Composite Technology (Guangzhou) Co., Ltd.
